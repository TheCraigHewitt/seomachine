# Brand Voice — Unifi.me

## Company Overview
Unifi is a stablecoin wallet developed by LINE NEXT, accessible directly through LINE Messenger and the web. Built on the Kaia blockchain, it offers deposits, custody, payments, transfers, and yield — all in one platform. Launched globally in March 2026.

## Voice Pillars

### 1. Approachable
- We make stablecoins feel normal, not technical
- Write as if explaining to a smart friend who doesn't know crypto
- Avoid jargon when a simpler word works (e.g., "earn interest" over "yield farming")

### 2. Trustworthy
- Non-custodial: users control their own assets
- Backed by LINE's ecosystem (200M+ MAU across Asia)
- Transparent about how interest is generated and risks involved

### 3. Practical
- Focus on real use cases: earning, sending, paying
- Every feature should connect to a tangible user benefit
- "What can I do with this?" should always be answered

## Default Language
- **Blog posts**: Korean (한국어) — all blog content is written in Korean by default unless explicitly requested otherwise

## Tone by Content Type
- **Blog posts**: Informative, conversational, confidence-building
- **Product updates**: Clear, concise, benefit-first
- **Educational content**: Patient, step-by-step, no assumptions about prior knowledge

## Core Messages
1. Earn interest on your stablecoins — starting at 4% APY with flexible deposits and withdrawals
2. No app download needed — access directly through LINE Messenger
3. You own your assets — non-custodial wallet with social login simplicity
4. Send money across borders — fast, low-cost stablecoin transfers

## Terminology Preferences
- Use "stablecoin" not "crypto" when referring to USDT/USDC
- Use "wallet" not "account"
- Use "earn" or "interest" not "yield" in user-facing content
- Use "send" not "remit" or "transfer" for cross-border payments
- "LINE NEXT" when referring to the parent company
- "Unifi" (not "UNIFI" or "UniFi") for the product name

## What We Are NOT
- We are not a speculative trading platform
- We are not a bank (but we offer bank-like convenience)
- We are not just another crypto wallet — we're a stablecoin-first platform embedded in a messaging app
